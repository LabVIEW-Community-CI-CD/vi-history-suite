## Context

What problem or force made a decision necessary? Describe the situation, the
constraints, and any prior approach being replaced. Keep it factual.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences, then the specifics. Say what was
chosen — and, where useful, what was explicitly rejected.

## Consequences

- The positive and negative results of the decision.
- Follow-on obligations, migrations, or superseded ADRs.
- What future work this enables or constrains.
